An entry for the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/f2pdfak6).

## The entry

One line of text, a roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after
midnight — the joke is the whole entry:

> At midnight the fridge lights up, shows me the cake, then locks the door — it's not a fridge, it's a museum with snacks.

## How it was picked

`main.py` holds ten fresh one-line candidates, each scored against the
judge brief's own criteria in its published order (on-subject, real
joke with a surprise turn, tightness, the midnight refusal itself as
the target, sounding spoken aloud at the door). The winner scored
71/75 with a clear margin over the 68/75 runner-up. Running `main.py`
writes the winning line to `/output/entry.txt`, validated against the
gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, single line).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
